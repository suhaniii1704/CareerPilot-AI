# 🚀 CareerPilot AI

### Your Personalized AI Career Coach

CareerPilot AI is an AI-powered career guidance platform designed to help students and job seekers improve their career readiness through **resume analysis, ATS evaluation, resume-based chat, job matching, AI-powered interview preparation, and personalized career roadmaps**.

The platform combines Generative AI, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), semantic search, and interactive Streamlit interfaces to provide personalized career guidance from a user's resume and career goals.

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 📄 AI Resume Analysis

Upload your resume and receive an AI-powered analysis including:

- Resume parsing
- ATS compatibility score
- Personal information extraction
- Education analysis
- Experience analysis
- Project analysis
- Technical skill extraction
- Certification extraction
- Missing skill identification
- AI-powered improvement suggestions

---

### 💬 Resume Chat

Interact with your resume using an AI-powered conversational assistant.

Features include:

- Resume-based question answering
- Context-aware responses
- Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)
- Semantic document search
- FAISS-based vector retrieval
- Conversational interaction with resume content

---

### 🎯 Job Match

Compare your resume with a specific job description.

The Job Match module provides:

- Resume-to-job-description matching
- Match score
- Missing keywords
- Skill-gap identification
- AI recommendations
- Job-specific improvement suggestions
- Downloadable job match report

---

### 🎤 AI Interview Coach

Practice interviews using an AI-powered interview assistant.

Features include:

- Interactive mock interviews
- Role-specific interview questions
- Resume-aware interview questions
- Conversational interview flow
- AI-powered answer evaluation
- Interview performance feedback
- Final interview evaluation
- Downloadable interview report

---

### 🧠 Personalized Career Roadmap

Generate a personalized career development roadmap based on:

- Resume
- Target role
- Current skill level
- Available preparation time

The roadmap supports:

- **1 Month** → 4 weeks
- **3 Months** → 12 weeks
- **6 Months** → 24 weeks

Each roadmap includes:

- Weekly learning objectives
- Practical tasks
- Skill gaps
- Career preparation activities
- Portfolio recommendations
- Final project recommendation

A downloadable PDF roadmap is also generated.

---

### 📑 Professional PDF Reports

CareerPilot AI generates structured PDF reports for:

- Resume Analysis
- Interview Performance
- Job Matching
- Career Roadmap

Reports use a clean card-based layout for easier reading and presentation.
